Privacy Policy

Sneaky Ninja (Android) · Effective date: 31 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sneaky Ninja Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Sneaky Ninja (package com.sneakyninjastudio.sneakyninja), available on Google Play. We designed the game to collect as little as possible: there is no account, no login, and we run no servers of our own. The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and those ads are the only reason any information leaves your device.

1. Information stored only on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: unlocked levels, stars, coins, outfits and upgrades, your daily-gift streak, win streak, rank, Star Chest progress, quests, and your sound, music, vibration and reminder settings. This data stays on your device. We never receive it. If you use Android's backup feature, Android may back up your game progress to your own Google account, under Google's control. We cannot access that backup.

2. Information collected by advertising partners

Sneaky Ninja uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show ads, including rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for and store your consent where the law requires it. These Google services automatically collect and share the following information with Google:

Google encrypts this data in transit (TLS). Google acts as an independent controller of this data. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and How Google uses data for advertising.

The game does not ask for and does not access your name, email, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise (GPS) location. We do not sell personal information for money.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the first time you open the game, you may see a consent form from Google. You can change your choice at any time through Settings → Ad privacy settings in the game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • US state privacy rights: where showing personalised ads counts as "selling" or "sharing" personal information under laws such as the CCPA/CPRA, you can opt out through the device ad settings above, or through the in-game privacy options where they are shown.
  • Reminder notifications: if you allow notifications, the game sets local reminders on your phone (for example, that your daily gift is ready), at most three a day and never at night. They are created on your device from your saved game progress; no data is sent anywhere to produce them. Turn them off in the game (Settings → Reminders) or in your device's notification settings.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the game stops all further collection.

4. Retention and deletion

Your game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Sneaky Ninja → Storage → Clear storage). Both delete it permanently. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies. You can delete your advertising ID at any time as described above.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where GDPR applies, personalised advertising relies on your consent, collected through Google's consent form. Non-personalised or limited ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention may rely on legitimate interests in funding a free game and keeping ads safe. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are best directed to Google. We will help where we can.

6. Children

Sneaky Ninja is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow the collection of personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, please contact us and we will help.

7. Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Data sent by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the data involved to a minimum.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the ad SDK changes. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
